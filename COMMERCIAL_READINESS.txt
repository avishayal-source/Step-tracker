--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
MUST-HAVE BEFORE RELEASE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Play Store assets
    * App icon (adaptive, 512px)
    * Feature graphic
    * Screenshots (phone + tablet)

- Privacy policy
    * Required for apps using location and activity recognition.
    * Must be hosted at a public URL and linked in the Play Console listing.

- Release build
    * Enable minifyEnabled true
    * Add ProGuard / R8 rules
    * Sign with a release keystore (keep the keystore + passwords backed up safely)

- Background tracking
    * The current wake lock is deprecated.
    * Switch to WorkManager or a proper foreground service with a persistent
      notification that survives battery optimization (Doze / App Standby).

- Onboarding flow
    * First-launch permissions explanation.
    * Google requires a clear rationale for ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION and location.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT QUALITY IMPROVEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- FusedLocationProviderClient (Google Play Services)
    * Replace raw LocationManager.
    * Significantly better GPS accuracy; handles indoor/outdoor transitions.

- Unit tests
    * Especially for the classifier and distance calculation.

- Crash reporting
    * Firebase Crashlytics (or similar).

- Accessibility
    * Content descriptions for screen readers.
    * Sufficient color contrast and touch target sizes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NICE-TO-HAVE / DIFFERENTIATION
