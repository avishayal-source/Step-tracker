1. DATA SAFETY FORM (Play Console > App content > Data safety)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Google defines "collection" as data sent OFF the device. Y Walk processes
everything on-device and transmits nothing, so the answers are minimal.

Q: Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
   -> NO. All data is processed on the device and is not sent to us or any
      third party.

Q: Is all of the user data encrypted in transit?
   -> Not applicable (no data is transmitted off the device).

Q: Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?
   -> Yes. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data. Users can also clear
      session data from the Tracking tab.

Notes to keep on file (in case Google asks how permissions are used):
• Physical activity / activity recognition: used on-device to count steps and
  classify walking vs jogging vs running. Not transmitted.
• Location (optional, fine/coarse): used on-device ONLY during active tracking
  to improve distance and pace accuracy. No location/route history is stored or
  sent. The app works without it.
• Notifications: local workout reminders only.
• Health profile entered in the coach (age, sex, weight, height, goals): stored
  in encrypted preferences on the device; never transmitted.

If at any point you add analytics, crash reporting, ads, or cloud features,
this form and the Privacy Policy MUST be updated before that release.

3. STORE LISTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
App name (max 30 chars):
  Y Walk

Short description (max 80 chars):
  Private step, walk & run tracker with a personal coach — all on your device.

Full description (max 4000 chars):
  Y Walk is a privacy-first fitness tracker that counts your steps and tells
  walking, jogging and running apart automatically — no account, no sign-up,
  and your data never leaves your phone.

  WHAT YOU GET
  • Accurate step counting with on-device walk / jog / run detection.
  • Distance and pace, refined by optional GPS while you track.
  • A session timeline so you can see how your workout unfolded.
  • Workout history kept locally on your device.
  • Botty, a personal training coach that builds a simple, realistic plan from
    your goal and adds dated workouts and reminders to your schedule.
  • Step-length calibration for a more personal distance estimate.

  PRIVACY FIRST
  • Works fully offline. The app does not transmit your workout data to us.
  • No ads, no trackers, no third-party analytics.
  • Cloud backup and device transfer of app data are disabled by design.
  • Uninstalling removes all app data.

  PERMISSIONS
  • Physical activity — to count steps and detect your activity.
  • Location (optional) — improves distance/pace while tracking; the app works
    without it and stores no route history.
  • Notifications (optional) — workout reminders for your plan.

  IMPORTANT — NOT A MEDICAL APP
  Y Walk is for informational and fitness purposes only. It is not a medical
  device and does not provide medical advice. Coaching and AI-generated
  recommendations are informational only and do not replace professional
  medical or fitness advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional before
  beginning any exercise program, especially if you have a medical condition or
  experience symptoms such as chest pain, dizziness, or shortness of breath.
