Y WALK — WEAR OS PLAY STORE
===========================
Date: 2026-09-18

Signing: same release keystore as the phone AAB
Artifact: wear/build/outputs/bundle/release/wear-release.aab
versionCode: 10003   (must stay unique vs phone codes)
versionName: 1.0.2

Do NOT create a second Play app. Add a Wear OS form factor to the existing
Y Walk listing, then upload this AAB on the Wear OS-only track.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAY CONSOLE — ADD WEAR OS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the existing Y Walk app (com.ywalk.app).
2. Test and release → Advanced settings → Form factors → Add form factor
   → Wear OS.
3. Upload at least one Wear OS screenshot (round watch, app running).
   Capture from Android Studio (Wear emulator or the Galaxy Watch):
   home with due workout, live workout (timer/steps), Saved screen.
   Typical size: 384×384 or 420×420 PNG; Play accepts Wear screenshots
   from the Console Wear form-factor flow.
4. Opt in to Wear OS and accept the Wear review policy.
5. Create a release on the Wear OS closed/internal test track first
   (Play requires closed testing before Wear production rollout).
6. Upload wear-release.aab. Use the SAME upload key as the phone app.
7. After a test release exists, Start rollout to production when ready.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LISTING COPY (must include the words "Wear OS")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Play rejects Wear apps whose store listing never says "Wear OS".

Add to the existing listing short or full description:

  Also on Wear OS: start today's walk/run from your watch. Workouts
  continue if you lower your wrist, then sync to History on the phone.

Watch-specific "what's new" for this first Wear upload:

  • Start today's scheduled workout or a free session on Wear OS
  • Steps, timer and distance on the watch
  • Whistle + vibration at walk/run changes
  • Auto-sync finished sessions to Y Walk on the phone


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. DATA SAFETY / PERMISSIONS (Wear form factor)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Same answers as the phone listing — nothing leaves the device.

Wear uses:
  • Activity recognition / body sensors — step counting while a workout runs
  • Notifications — ongoing workout notification
  • Foreground service (Health) — keep tracking when the screen is off
  • No location on the watch (stride-only in this version)
  • No account, no ads, no cloud

Privacy policy URL (same as phone):
  https://github.com/avishayal-source/Docs/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AFTER UPLOAD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Uninstall the debug Y Walk from the watch before installing from Play
(same package name; Play will not overlay a differently signed debug build).
Pair with the Play phone build so schedule + History sync keep working.
