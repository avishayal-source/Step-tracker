Y WALK — WEAR OS MVP SPEC
==========================
Status: Wear MVP M1–M5 on commercial/legal-compliance (Play Wear AAB)
Date: 2026-07-20 (updated 2026-07-30)
Branch: feature/wear-os-workout (from development)

Product split
-------------
WATCH (during workout, phone may stay home)
  - Today's synced schedule
  - Start / finish workout
  - Offline activity tracking
  - Local storage until phone reconnects

PHONE (before / after)
  - Botty, plan approval, reminders
  - History, insights, calibration
  - Sync schedule → watch; import finished sessions ← watch

Out of scope (MVP)
------------------
  - Heart rate
  - Apple Watch / Garmin / Fitbit SDKs
  - Botty / goal evaluation on watch
  - Reminder scheduling on watch (phone notifications may still appear on watch via system)
  - Full History / insights UI on watch
  - Low-battery warning UI on watch


────────────────────────────────────────
DECISIONS (from product Q&A)
────────────────────────────────────────
1. Free workouts: YES — scheduled OR ad-hoc Start on watch
2. Schedule sync horizon: TODAY ONLY
3. Distance: stride-based estimate from phone calibration ONLY in MVP
   - Watch GPS: OUT of MVP (confirmed 2026-07-20) — candidate for v1.1 / stretch
4. Classification: SIMPLER on-watch detector (not full phone port)
5. Watch UI during workout: activity type, steps, timer, distance, current segment + progress, next segment preview
6. Sync after Finish: automatic when phone is nearby (no extra tap)
7. Unsynced sessions: keep on watch until sync (no N-day nag in MVP)
8. Git branch: feature/wear-os-workout off development
9. If phone present mid-workout: prefer phone GPS for distance when available (hybrid)
10. Watch launcher name: "Y Walk"
